Orange Juice Recipe

Description

This is a delicious and fresh Orange Juice recipe..

Ingredients

  • 1 (16 ounce) jar Alfredo-style pasta sauce
  • 1 (16 ounce) package angel hair pasta
  • 2 pounds fresh shrimp, peeled and deveined

Directions

  1. In a saucepan over low-temperature, heat the Alfredo sauce.
    Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil.
    Add pasta and cook for 8 minutes or until al dente; drain.
